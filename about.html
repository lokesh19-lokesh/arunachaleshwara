Welcome to Our Sannidhi

Serving Pilgrims with Devotion and Care

Located at Avulpet Bypass in the holy town of Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu, Arunachaleshwara Tours & Travels was established to cater to the spiritual, logistical, and transport needs of pilgrims visiting the sacred abode of Lord Arunachaleswarar.

Tiruvannamalai is one of the most sacred pilgrimage centers in India, revered as the Agni Sthalam—representing the Fire element among the five universal elements (Pancha Bhootha Sthalams). Thousands of devotees travel each month to perform the sacred 14-kilometer Girivalam circumambulation of Arunachala Hill and seek blessings at the historic temple.

We understand that a pilgrimage is deeply personal and sacred. Our primary objective is to remove all travel anxiety—arranging spotless accommodation, punctual vehicle pickups, and guided temple visits—so you can immerse yourself completely in prayers and meditation.

Founder's Story

A Sacred Journey of Devotion & Service

"Travel and devotion have been my life's passion. Since college, I've worked in the travel business and visited most of India's famous sacred sites. I also owned and ran a South Indian restaurant, where I learned that true hospitality means anticipating what people need."

"I came to Arunachalam with a bag of clothes and a heart full of love for this temple and hill. For several months I explored, observed, and watched how travelers moved through Thiruvannamalai, where they struggled, and what they needed most."

"I opened a small guidance and information office, and it grew with the help of my network and the needs of travelers. Today, this website carries that purpose forward: a place to plan your visit, navigate with ease, and spend your time here in peace."

Mission, Vision & Values

Guided by principles of spiritual hospitality, honesty, and safety.

Our Mission

To provide seamless, comfortable, and affordable pilgrimage travel services in and around Tiruvannamalai, enabling every devotee to experience a peaceful and uninterrupted spiritual journey.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the most trusted, dependable, and hospitable pilgrimage travel partner across Tamil Nadu, celebrated for our ethics, warm service, and dedication to spiritual travelers.

Our Values

Devotion (Bhakti): Serving pilgrims as our sacred duty.
Integrity: Clear rates without surprise charges.
Respect: Honor for temple traditions, rituals, and fellow pilgrims.
