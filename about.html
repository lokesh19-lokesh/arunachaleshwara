Welcome to Our Sannidhi

Serving Pilgrims with Devotion and Care

Located at Avulpet Bypass in the holy town of Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu, Arunachaleshwara Tours & Travels was established to cater to the spiritual, logistical, and transport needs of pilgrims visiting the sacred abode of Lord Arunachaleswarar.

Tiruvannamalai is one of the most sacred pilgrimage centers in India, revered as the Agni Sthalam—representing the Fire element among the five universal elements (Pancha Bhootha Sthalams). Thousands of devotees travel each month to perform the sacred 14-kilometer Girivalam circumambulation of Arunachala Hill and seek blessings at the historic temple.

We understand that a pilgrimage is deeply personal and sacred. Our primary objective is to remove all travel anxiety—arranging spotless accommodation, punctual vehicle pickups, and guided temple visits—so you can immerse yourself completely in prayers and meditation.
