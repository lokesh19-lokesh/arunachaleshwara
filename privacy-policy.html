1. Introduction

At Arunachaleshwara Tours & Travels ("we," "our," or "us"), located at Avulpet Bypass, Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu, we value the trust you place in us when sharing your travel requirements. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, and safeguard your personal details when you visit our website or communicate with us through forms, calls, or WhatsApp.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information voluntarily submitted by you to facilitate your pilgrimage travel bookings and customer support:

  • Contact Details: Full name, telephone number, mobile number, and email address.
  • Pilgrimage & Travel Logistics: Preferred travel dates, number of travelers (adults and children), pickup location, destination shrines, and special requirements (such as elder assistance or AC preferences).
  • Technical Data: Basic website analytics (e.g. browser type, pages viewed, device type) used purely to ensure responsiveness and technical stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the details you share exclusively for the following operational purposes:

  • To generate accurate quotes for room bookings, taxi rentals, and tour packages.
  • To communicate itinerary updates, driver details, and pickup confirmations via WhatsApp, SMS, or phone.
  • To assist with temple darshan and Girivalam arrangements tailored to your schedule.
  • To address your questions and provide 24/7 travel assistance during your stay in Tiruvannamalai.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal information to third parties or marketing agencies. Your contact details are only shared with our verified drivers, accommodation managers, or transport operators strictly on a need-to-know basis to fulfill your booked travel arrangements.

5. Data Security

We employ standard security protocols to safeguard your contact information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Note that initial website enquiries generated via WhatsApp are governed by WhatsApp’s end-to-end encrypted messaging infrastructure.

6. Cookies & Third-Party Services

Our website may utilize lightweight technical cookies to remember UI preferences (e.g., list vs. grid view). We may embed maps from Google Maps to help you locate our office and sacred temple sites. These third-party services operate under their respective privacy policies.

7. Your Rights

You may at any time request to review, correct, or delete any personal contact details provided to us by contacting our office.
