How Manual Room Booking Works

  1. Select your room category and click Book Now.
  2. Fill in your check-in/check-out dates and guest details to view the estimated amount.
  3. Click Submit Booking Request to receive your unique Booking ID.
  4. Click Contact on WhatsApp to instantly share your booking ID with our accommodation desk.
  5. Our coordinator verifies room availability and shares official UPI / Bank Transfer details.
  6. Once your payment is manually verified, your room number is allocated and your official PDF invoice is shared on WhatsApp.
